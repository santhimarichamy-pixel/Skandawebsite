Technical Engineering FAQ

What engineering standards do reconditioned trays follow? +
All processing runs strictly comply with JEDEC JEP95 standards. Every individual tray goes through high-resolution optical coplanarity sorting to eliminate warp and pocket twist limits down to ±0.08mm limits, ensuring perfect pickup performance for automated robots.
How are premium trays validated against electrostatic discharge (ESD)? +
Trays are subjected to multi-point surface resistivity analysis via standard double-probe megohmmeter tracking. We strictly certify static-dissipative values between 10⁵ and 10¹¹ ohms/sq. Verification readouts are etched into every structural shipment.
