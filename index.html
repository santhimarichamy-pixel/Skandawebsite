The Opportunity

Your empty tray may not be waste.

Semiconductor operations continuously handle packaging materials. Depending on the application, some empty trays may be candidates for inspection, reconditioning and controlled reuse rather than immediate disposal.

Existing trays

Instead of automatically treating every returned or empty tray as scrap, eligible trays can be evaluated against defined dimensional, material, cleanliness, ESD and application requirements.

New tray demand

When reconditioning is not appropriate, Skanda can support new standard or custom tray requirements based on the customer's specifications.

Circular Tray Program

Collect. Inspect. Recondition. Qualify. Reuse.

Start with a controlled pilot using your existing trays. The objective is to establish whether a repeatable reconditioning and reuse process is suitable for your application.

1

Collect

Collect eligible empty trays from the customer's operation.

2

Inspect

Review dimensions, physical condition and application requirements.

3

Recondition

Clean and restore trays according to the defined process.

4

Qualify

Evaluate samples against customer-defined acceptance criteria.

5

Reuse

Establish an ongoing collection and supply program where requirements are met.

New Manufacturing

Engineering & Quality

Qualification starts with the specification.

Semiconductor packaging applications can have strict requirements. Our evaluation process is designed around the customer's actual tray specification and operating environment.

Dimensions

Tray geometry, pocket dimensions, tolerances and overall dimensional requirements.

ESD

Material and electrostatic-control requirements defined by the intended application.

Cleanliness

Cleaning and handling requirements established for the customer's process.

Thermal

Temperature, baking and environmental requirements considered during qualification.

Sustainability

Move from single-use thinking toward circular supply.

A reconditioning program can create a controlled path for eligible packaging trays to remain in productive use longer.

Skanda's approach is pilot-first: measure the condition, define acceptance criteria, validate the process, and then determine whether ongoing reuse is appropriate.

A practical circular model

Collect eligible trays from the supply chain.

Inspect and recondition them using a defined process.

Validate samples against the customer's requirements.

Return qualified trays to controlled use where appropriate.

Track quantities and program performance over time.
