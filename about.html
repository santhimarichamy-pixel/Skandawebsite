Our Core Mission

SkandaTechnology provides premier semiconductor parts manufacturing and circular packaging logistics for advanced SMT lines. We optimize high-volume component pipelines by delivering high-reliability carrier arrays built to uncompromising engineering tolerances.

JEDEC Matrix & ESD Rigor

Every packaging module distributed or reconditioned by SkandaTechnology adheres strictly to JEDEC JEP95 mechanical outline dimensions. Our validation process guarantees surface resistivity parameters between 10⁵ and 10¹¹ ohms/sq, ensuring complete protection against electrostatic field failure.

The 5-Year Horizon Target

Our long-term operational objective is absolute integration within tier-1 gigafactory component ecosystems, positioning SkandaTechnology to become a primary certified semiconductor parts infrastructure partner for Tesla Terrafab within a 5-year roadmap via resilient, scalable execution.
